# SlateCheck Minimum-Overhead Profile

Version 0.2.0

## 1. Purpose

This profile is the default adoption path for small teams and routine creative-production uses. It preserves SlateCheck's evidence, authority and scope boundaries while keeping administration proportionate.

The operating principle is **assess once, reuse while unchanged**. A qualified person establishes a bounded decision or standing approval. Each covered use then needs a short confirmation. Specialist review returns only when a relevant fact, authority, environment or condition changes.

## 2. Four responsibility functions

SlateCheck assigns four functions within the adopter's existing jobs and organisational structure:

| Function | Minimum responsibility |
|---|---|
| Proposer | Describes the intended use and reports changes. |
| Decision owner | Owns the recorded outcome, routes unresolved questions and keeps the next action clear. |
| Authorised authority | Supplies the specific rights, privacy, security, workforce or external decision required by a gate. |
| Operator | Confirms that the actual use remains within scope and that required conditions are complete. |

One person may perform several functions. Existing producers, legal advisers, privacy or security contacts and production staff can perform them through current processes. An external client, contributor or other rights holder can be the authorised authority for a decision only they can make.

The record identifies the function exercised and the authority behind it. Each adopter retains its existing job titles and decision structures.

## 3. Start with one bounded use

A minimum-overhead adoption begins with one common, low-variation use rather than an organisation-wide inventory. For example: summarising ordinary production meetings for the same show in one approved enterprise account.

The team:

1. describes the covered source class, operation, target use, environment and exclusions;
2. verifies the applicable authority and conditions once;
3. issues a bounded decision or standing approval with an owner, expiry and change triggers;
4. gives users the five-question routine check below; and
5. measures actual requester and specialist time before expanding.

Additional productions, tools and source classes are added when there is a real use for them. Existing systems remain the source of truth for contracts, identities, assets and tool approvals; SlateCheck stores stable references and the verified findings relied upon.

## 4. Five-question routine check

A use covered by a current standing approval asks:

1. Are the material, represented people and model artifacts within the approved source class?
2. Is the AI operation, including anything retained, exactly within scope?
3. Are the purpose, destination and audience within scope?
4. Is the approved tool, account and configuration being used?
5. Is the standing approval current, with its prerequisites complete and its change triggers absent?

Five confirmed answers create a dated `ready` event linked to the standing approval and the exact material or manifest. The operator then follows the recorded conditions. The organisation can combine the confirmation and ready event in one form, row or production-system action. The [routine-use record](../examples/minimum-overhead-routine-use.md) is a copyable template.

Any uncertain or changed answer creates a targeted hold for that question. The decision owner routes only the affected gate to the relevant authority. Unchanged gates retain their existing basis.

## 5. Minimum records

The profile can operate with three controlled record sets:

| Record | Minimum content |
|---|---|
| Standing approvals | Bounded material and model source classes, people or verified cohort, operation and persistence, target use, environment, exclusions, conditions, authorities, validity and change triggers. |
| Uses | Time, proposer, decision owner, exact material or manifest reference, standing approval reference and five-question confirmation. |
| Events | Ready, started, suspended and closed status; completed conditions; release or deletion evidence where applicable. |

Records may be rows in a protected sheet or fields in an existing production system. Evidence can remain in an authoritative repository when the SlateCheck record retains a stable reference, version, verified finding and access route sufficient for reconstruction.

Initial adoption uses existing owners and repositories. It omits a separate AI office, per-use committee meetings, duplicate contract storage, a complete enterprise asset inventory and machine-readable JSON. Larger or more automated implementations can add capabilities as their volume and risk justify them.

## 6. Escalation triggers

The routine path ends when the proposal includes a material change or unresolved fact, including:

- a new source class, contributor, agreement or collective term;
- a different production, beneficiary, audience or external distribution;
- training, fine-tuning, a reusable capability or another newly retained artefact;
- a new tool, account, model, region, provider term or data-handling setting;
- sensitive personal, workforce, performance, health or biometric information;
- an exception, dispute, withdrawal, incident, expired basis or failed condition; or
- a proposal outside the standing approval's recorded boundaries.

Escalation can still be narrow. A tool-setting change routes to the person authorised to verify the environment. An unclear performer restriction routes to the relevant rights authority. Other established findings remain available unless the change affects them.

## 7. Operating targets

The pilot sets local targets and records actual results. A useful initial target for an unchanged routine use is:

- five minutes or less of proposer and operator time;
- no new specialist review;
- one short confirmation record; and
- immediate identification of the responsible authority when escalation occurs.

Initial assessment, rule preparation and evidence verification are measured separately from repeat-use effort. A standing approval that fails to reduce repeat effort should be simplified, narrowed or retired.

## 8. Relationship to conformance

This profile is a Level 1 operating pattern. It organises the required controls economically while preserving the positive basis needed for a permitted decision. The standing approval contains the full bounded assessment; the routine check confirms that the live use still matches it.

Uses outside a current standing approval follow the normal assessment process. Organisations can add managed queues, shared registries, automated matching and independent assurance as their case volume and risk justify Levels 2–4.
