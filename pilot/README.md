# SCOPE Five-Case Usability Smoke Pilot

Run this smoke pilot before the larger comparative protocol in
[`docs/PILOT_EVALUATION.md`](../docs/PILOT_EVALUATION.md). It tests whether a
requester can submit a real AI-use question, understand the answer and move a
held request toward resolution at reasonable cost.

SCOPE is being evaluated. Existing authorised processes continue to control
real work, and no pilot result grants permission.

## Working rule: specification soft freeze

During the five cases, change the specification or prototype only to:

- correct a safety, privacy, security or factual defect;
- repair a blocking usability defect observed in a session; or
- capture information that changes an outcome, owner or next action.

Record proposed changes in [`RESULTS.md`](RESULTS.md). Defer new services,
integrations, schemas, scenarios and general polish until the case-5 review.

## Questions

1. Can a requester describe and confirm a proposal without specialist help?
2. Can the organisation locate and verify the evidence needed to assess it?
3. Can the requester explain the outcome, next owner and permitted next action?
4. Can at least one held request be resolved or narrowed through reassessment?
5. Is the answer worth the requester and specialist effort?

## Participants

Recruit at least two requesters from production, creative or technology who did
not write the framework, the evidence owners or authorities needed for the
cases, and one facilitator. One person may hold several organisational roles;
record each role separately. Do not use results as employee-performance data.

## Select five cases

Use current real work where it can be handled safely. De-identify the pilot
record and keep source media, contract text and personal information in their
controlled systems.

| Case | Required shape | What it tests |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Routine same-production use with a likely standing basis | Friction on an ordinary request |
| 2 | Incomplete or poorly bounded material selection | Efficient discovery of missing inventory |
| 3 | Contributor, client or licensed-material ambiguity | Whether a hold becomes a specific request |
| 4 | Tool, account, region or retention mismatch | Whether environment controls stay independent and understandable |
| 5 | High-impact, restricted or clearly unacceptable use | Whether the boundary and a safer alternative are understood |

Include at least one case with an existing-process decision and one case the
requester genuinely wants to progress. Register the five cases in `RESULTS.md`
before the first session.

## Run each session

Allow 30–45 minutes and use the case template in `RESULTS.md`.

### 1. Unassisted intake

Give the requester the prototype and this prompt:

> Use SCOPE to ask whether you can carry out this proposed AI use. Stop when you
> receive a decision brief.

Record time, uncertainty, guessed fields, terminology problems, sensitive-data
handling, bypass attempts and facilitator interventions. Avoid coaching unless
the requester becomes completely blocked.

### 2. Comprehension check

Hide the brief and ask:

1. What can you do now?
2. What must wait or stop?
3. Who acts next?
4. What information or approval is needed?
5. What change requires reassessment?

Rate each response against the issued brief and preserve the participant's
wording when it reveals a usability problem.

### 3. Evidence and decision work

The relevant stewards and authorities process the case. Record active minutes
for evidence location, missing-fact preparation, interpretation, data entry and
decision review. Record waiting time separately and preserve uncertainty.

### 4. Resolution trace

For every hold, record one next action, owner, required evidence and completion
test. Take at least one genuine held case from assigned work through verified
response and reassessment. If it cannot progress, retain the external blocker
and owner rather than simulating success.

## Initial thresholds

Confirm these hypotheses with the pilot authorities before seeing results.

Expansion pauses while any of these remains unexplained:

- permission despite a verified prohibition or missing prerequisite;
- a decision that cannot be reconstructed from its stated evidence;
- an unresolved item presented as verified; or
- a hold, condition or prohibition without an accountable owner.

Usability targets are:

- median unassisted intake time of 3 minutes or less;
- in at least 4 of 5 case sessions, the requester identifies the immediate action and next owner;
- every hold identifies a specific missing fact, approval or condition;
- no requester treats saving a brief as permission; and
- the held case reaches reassessment or retains a documented external blocker.

Report actual staff minutes alongside the current-process baseline. Flag any
routine case that takes materially longer without discovering a relevant fact,
obligation or inconsistency.

## Review after case 5

Classify proposed changes as:

- **Keep** — changed a decision or prevented a material error.
- **Simplify** — mattered but was difficult to supply or understand.
- **Remove or defer** — did not affect the cases and has no independent safety requirement.

Choose whether to proceed to the comparative pilot, revise and repeat the smoke
pilot, or suspend implementation pending resolution of a cost, authority or
evidence problem. Record the evidence, dissent, owner and next review date.
